An Italian Halloween Story

The Story

It was Halloween night in San Pier Niceto, Sicily.

Sixteen-year-old Luca was staying with his grandparents when his Nonna gave him one warning:

“Whatever you hear tonight, don't go near the old church.”

Luca laughed. “Why?”

She looked at him seriously.

“Because on Halloween, the shadow comes back.”

That night, Luca was walking home with his cousin Sofia when they heard a bell ring in the distance.

BOOOONG.

They stopped.

“There isn't supposed to be a bell ringing this late,” Sofia whispered.

Then they saw it.

A tall, dark figure standing at the end of the empty street.

It didn't move.

Luca blinked.

The figure was gone.

Suddenly, they heard footsteps behind them.

They ran toward home, but the footsteps followed.

Faster.

Faster.

When Luca finally looked back, nobody was there.

Only a dark shadow stretched across the road.

The next morning, Luca found strange footprints outside his bedroom window.

His grandmother saw them and went pale.

“It found you,” she whispered.

“What found me?”

She pointed toward the church.

“The Shadow of San Pier Niceto.”

That night, at exactly midnight, Luca heard three knocks on his bedroom door.

Knock.

Knock.

Knock.

A whisper came from the other side.

“Luca…”

He held his breath.

Then the voice whispered:

“Don't look behind you.”

Luca slowly turned.

A tall shadow was standing on the wall behind him.

And then, the shadow smiled.

THE END... OR IS IT?

When the bells ring at midnight, stay away from the church.
