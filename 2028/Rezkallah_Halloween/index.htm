The Room Beneath the Courtyard In the old quarter of Damascus, there are houses built around courtyards where orange trees grow between fountains and pale stone walls. One such house belonged to a family named Haddad. The house had been empty for almost twenty years. People in the neighborhood said the family had left suddenly one winter. No one could agree why. Some said the father had died. Others claimed the family had heard voices coming from beneath the house. Then, one summer, a young man named Sami bought it. Sami laughed when his neighbors warned him. “It's an empty house,” he said. “Not a haunted one.” The first few nights were peaceful. On the fifth night, Sami woke to the sound of water dripping.
