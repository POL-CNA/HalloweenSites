Pumpkin Drawing The House at the End of the Road

Happy Halloween

Halloween was always my favorite night of the year. I loved dressing up, hanging out with my friends, and getting as much candy as possible.

One Halloween night, my friends Tyler, Emma, Noah, and I were walking around the neighborhood. After getting candy from almost every house, Tyler pointed toward an old house at the end of the street.

“Have you guys ever been there?” he asked.

We all shook our heads. The house looked abandoned, with broken windows and trees covering the front yard.

Tyler dared us to go inside, so we walked up to the front door. He knocked, but nobody answered. Then the door slowly opened by itself.

We looked at each other, but Tyler walked inside. We followed him because none of us wanted to stay outside alone.

The house was dark and dusty. We walked through the hallway until we heard footsteps coming from upstairs.

“Maybe we should leave,” Emma whispered.

Before we could turn around, the front door slammed shut.

We ran upstairs and found a small bedroom. There was an old notebook sitting on a desk. I opened it and read the last sentence.

“If you're reading this, you're already trapped.”

Suddenly, the lights went out.

Someone whispered our names from the hallway.

We ran back downstairs, and this time the front door opened. We sprinted out of the house and didn't stop until we reached my house.

The next morning, we went back to see if the house was still there.

It wasn't.

There was only an empty field.

We thought maybe we had imagined everything until I found the old notebook in my backpack.

I opened it to the last page.

A new sentence had appeared.

“See you next Halloween.”
