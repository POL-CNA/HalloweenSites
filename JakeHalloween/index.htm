🎃 Story Idea: The Haunted Cedar Forest Every Halloween, people in a small village in Lebanon tell stories about a mysterious forest hidden high in the mountains.

They say that deep inside the forest stands an ancient cedar tree that nobody is supposed to touch.

One Halloween night, a 13-year-old boy named Caleb visits Lebanon with his family. While exploring near the mountains, Caleb and his friends hear a strange sound coming from the forest.

They decide to follow it.

As they walk between the tall cedar trees, they find an old stone path leading deeper into the forest. At the end of the path is a small, abandoned house.

Inside, they find an old wooden table covered in dusty photographs. Caleb picks one up.

shows three children standing in the forest. But something is wrong.

One of the children looks exactly like Caleb. Suddenly, the door slams shut.

The wind outside begins to howl, and the lights in the house flicker. Then Caleb hears a whisper from behind him.

"You shouldn't have come back."

Caleb turns around. Nobody is there.

His friends suddenly notice something terrifying the photographs on the table are changing. One by one, the pictures begin showing Caleb and his friends inside the house.

Then the clock on the wall begins to strike midnight. Caleb grabs his friends and runs toward the door.

But before they escape, he looks back at the old photograph. The three children are gone.

And written underneath the picture are four words: "We are free now." 👻🎃

Caleb and his friends race out of the forest, never looking back.

But when Caleb gets home, he finds something in his pocket. A small cedar leaf.

And on it, written in tiny letters, is his name.

We are free now... 👻 Caleb, the forest has chosen you!
